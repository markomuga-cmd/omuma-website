Crypto's compliance clock: the 4 November VASP licensing deadline

Existing virtual asset firms have until 4 November 2026 to get licensed under Kenya's new regime.

Kenya's Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, 2025 commenced on 4 November 2025 and gave existing operators one year to get licensed. That year runs out on 4 November 2026.

The Virtual Asset Service Providers Regulations, 2026, gazetted on 22 July 2026, set the framework. The Central Bank licenses payment and stability activities, including stablecoin issuers, while the Capital Markets Authority licenses exchanges, brokers and investment activities. The rules reach any firm providing virtual asset services in or from Kenya, foreign platforms serving Kenyan customers included, with capital thresholds from KES 10 million up to KES 300 million for stablecoin issuers.

If you run or advise an exchange, wallet, broker or token project that touches Kenya, licensing is a live deadline, not a future one. Map your activities to the right category, confirm which regulator you answer to, and move now.
