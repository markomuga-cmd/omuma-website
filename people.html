Partners

John Maurice Otieno Omuga

Managing Partner

An Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, a Notary Public and Commissioner for Oaths, with over thirty years in practice. His work centres on corporate law, banking and securities, capital markets, company restructuring and pensions.

Mark G. Omuga

Partner

An Advocate of the High Court. He holds an LLM in International Law from the University of Nairobi and a master's in Tax and Customs Administration, is pursuing an MBA in Strategic Management and completing the Certified Public Secretary qualification, and is an associate member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators, Kenya. He is the author and co-author of the firm's texts, and practises across financial services and payments, real estate, insurance regulation, corporate and commercial law, employment and tax.
