Practice areas

A full-service practice, led where it is deepest.

Where our depth is

Two fields we know better than most.

Payments & financial services

Regulatory, licensing and compliance advice across digital financial services and fintech, from digital lending and payment solutions to card and electronic payment systems and data protection, for banks, fintechs, credit providers and payment service providers.

Real estate & project finance

Land across Kenya's tenure regimes, structuring and security, and the documentation from the SPV and the joint venture through to the lender and development-finance papers.

What we do

Financial services, payments & fintech
