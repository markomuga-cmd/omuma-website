About the firm

Full-service, with depth where it counts.

We serve private businesses, financial institutions, investors, developers and government, and we are known for depth in the areas our clients most need it.

Who we are

A Nairobi firm built on substance.

Omuma Advocates LLP is a full-service firm, so a client can bring the whole of a matter to one place. What sets us apart is depth rather than a thin spread: in financial services and payments, and in real estate and project finance, our partner has written the Kenyan texts other lawyers reach for.

We work the way clients tell us they want their lawyers to work. A partner stays on the matter from start to finish. The advice is commercial and plainly put, built to be acted on. And we price defined work at a fixed fee wherever we can, so the cost is known before the work begins.
