A wider net for lenders: the Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers Regulations, 2026

New rules extend Central Bank oversight beyond digital lenders to asset finance and buy-now-pay-later.

The Central Bank of Kenya (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations, 2026, published as Legal Notice 191 of 2026 on 29 September, widen Central Bank oversight beyond the digital credit providers regime to credit businesses operating digitally or otherwise. They expressly reach some asset-finance and buy-now-pay-later models.

Non-bank lenders now carry licensing and ongoing duties. App-based lenders must let customers opt out of marketing once a loan is repaid, and failing to keep annual fees current attracts a KES 1 million penalty.

If you run an asset-finance, buy-now-pay-later or other non-deposit lending business, review your licensing status and compliance framework against the new rules.

This is general information, not legal advice. Speak to us about how it affects you.
